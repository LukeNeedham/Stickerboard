StickerBoard · App Icon

Five directions for the new launcher icon

Current icon is the inherited EweSticker mascot on brand teal (#0fa3a2). Each concept below is built for Android's adaptive-icon system — a full-bleed background layer plus a foreground mark clear of the safe-zone edges — so any of them can be produced as real mipmap assets next.

Direction 1

Peel

The universal sticker cue: a die-cut square with the corner curling back. Reads instantly as "sticker" even at 24px, and the curl leaves an empty triangle you could later fill with a tiny brand mark.

Direction 2

Board

Takes the app's actual name literally: three stickers of different colors overlapping at slight angles, like a real sticker board. Most distinctive silhouette of the set, and scales well to a monochrome themed-icon shape.

home screen scale
Direction 3

Stack

A fanned stack of three sticker cards on one brand-teal background — calmer and more geometric than Board, closer to the app's existing teal identity. Good middle ground between "new" and "familiar."

home screen scale
Direction 4

Ewe Redux

Keeps the sheep mascot inherited from EweSticker but flattens it to simple geometric shapes instead of the current hand-drawn outline. Preserves brand history and warmth while looking modern at small sizes.

home screen scale
Direction 5

Mark

An abstract "S" built as a single ribbon stroke — reads as a sticker peel and a wordmark initial at once. The most minimal, brand-forward option; pairs well as a small monochrome mark in notifications and the keyboard switcher.
