StickerBoard is an Android keyboard for sending your own stickers, GIFs and short videos in any app, built from a folder of images you choose yourself.

<b>Features:</b>

<ul>
<li><b>Send stickers anywhere</b>: works as a normal Android keyboard, with an automatic PNG then share-sheet fallback for apps that don't support stickers directly. </li>
<li><b>Wide format support</b>: gif, png, webp, jpeg, heif and svg images, plus 3gpp, mp4, mkv and webm video. </li>
<li><b>Your own sticker folder</b>: point StickerBoard at any folder on your device and every sub-folder becomes a sticker pack, up to 4096 stickers in total. </li>
<li><b>Stays in sync</b>: pull down on the keyboard or the in-app Stickers page to re-scan your folder for changes. </li>
<li><b>Sticker gallery</b>: browse every pack, see stats on your collection, and add photos straight from your device's gallery into any pack. </li>
<li><b>Recently used</b>: stickers you've sent recently surface automatically at the top of the keyboard. </li>
<li><b>Built-in search</b>: a dedicated search view finds stickers by file or pack name as you type. </li>
<li><b>Pinch to zoom</b>: pinch or spread on the keyboard to change how many stickers show per row. </li>
<li><b>Resizable keyboard</b>: drag the handle at the top of the keyboard to resize it - your preferred height is remembered. </li>
<li><b>Long-press preview</b>: long-press any sticker for an enlarged preview before sending it. </li>
<li><b>Guided first-run setup</b>: a short onboarding flow gets your keyboard enabled and your sticker folder chosen with live progress feedback. </li>
<li><b>Follows your system theme</b>: a Material 3 UI that adapts to your device's light or dark theme. </li>
</ul>
